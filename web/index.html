fitstitch

Stopped and saved your run halfway by accident? Join the two activities back into one, as if you had paused instead of saving. No data is lost.

Your files stay on this device: everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

1. Export the parts

On Garmin Connect, open each activity, then ⚙ → Export Original (you get a .zip). On Strava, open each activity, then ⋯ → Export Original. Files from other apps work if they are .fit files.

        4. Replace the parts

        Delete the original parts on Garmin Connect and on Strava first: both reject an upload with the same start time as an existing activity. Kudos, comments, photos and titles on the parts are lost. Then upload the stitched file on Garmin Connect (Import Data). If it doesn't reach Strava on its own, upload it at strava.com/upload.
