fitstitch

Join two FIT files into one activity

Stopped and saved your run or ride halfway by accident? Join the two activities back into one, as if you had paused instead of saving. No data is lost.

Private: your files are never uploaded or stored

Your files stay on this device: everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

There is no server that could receive them: fitstitch runs entirely in your browser, and once the page has loaded it even works offline. Nothing is stored either, except your language choice. The source code is open for anyone to check.

1. Export the parts

On Garmin Connect, open each activity, then ⚙ → Export Original (you get a .zip). On Strava, open each activity, then ⋯ → Export Original. FIT files from other devices and apps (Suunto, Wahoo, Coros…) work too.

The phone apps can't export files: use the websites, on a computer or in your phone's browser (if it keeps opening the app, ask for the desktop site).

        4. Replace the parts

        Delete the original parts first, wherever they were uploaded: Garmin Connect, Strava and most other apps reject an activity with the same start time as an existing one. Kudos, comments, photos and titles on the parts are lost. Then upload the stitched file to Garmin Connect (Import Data), Strava or any other app that accepts FIT files. If your apps sync with each other, uploading it to one may be enough.
